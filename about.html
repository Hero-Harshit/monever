The Challenge

Why Monever exists ?

"People do not track their daily expenses effectively, which leads to poor budgeting. The goal was to create an application where users can add expenses, categorize them, and view their total spending, all in one simple place."

HiH Hackathon (Problem Statement #2)

Everything we track for you

₹
Expense Tracking

Log daily expenses with custom categories. Your last-used category is remembered so adding entries is fast.

📅
EMI Reminders

Add recurring EMIs with due dates and amounts. Mark them as paid with one tap, it logs the expense automatically.

⇌
Person Ledger

Track who you lent money to and who owes you. See your net balance at a glance and close transactions when settled.

📊
Visual Insights

A pie chart breaks down your monthly spending by category. Filter by any past month to see exactly where your money went.

⚠
Budget Alerts

Set a monthly budget ceiling. Monever warns you the moment you overshoot, so surprises stay out of your bank statement.

↕
Easy Import & Exports

Your data never gets locked in. Export everything as JSON and import it back anytime, full control, zero dependency on a server.

🗝️
Fully Private, Fully Yours

We believe in your privacy. Your data stays with you it's never shared, never sold, fully under your control.

One day. Five phases.

0–2 hrs
Problem Breakdown

Analyzed the problem statement. Mapped out the three core user needs: tracking, categorizing, and visualizing.

2–6 hrs
UI Scaffolding

Built the HTML structure, navbar, forms, and tab layout using Bootstrap 5.3. Set up localStorage data model.

6–14 hrs
Core Logic

Wrote all JavaScript. add/delete/render for expenses, EMIs, and ledger. Implemented Chart.js pie chart.

14–20 hrs
Features & Polish

Added budget alerts, monthly filter, EMI mark-paid, export/import, and toast notifications.

20–24 hrs
Final Touches

Refined typography, responsive layout, color theme, and tested edge cases before submission.

Made with focus and very little sleep.
